# Prove one business before building SaaS infrastructure

Status: accepted

V0–V6 optimize for RJ Studio as the only business so the team can reach measurable end-to-end value within 20 days. Designs must remain migratable to a future `tenant_id`, but tenant routing, isolation, administration, and generalized onboarding are deferred until V7 because building them earlier would slow validation and encode requirements that have not been learned.
